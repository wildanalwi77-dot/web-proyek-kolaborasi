How Recycled Electronic Components Are Transformed into a Mini Bluetooth Speaker

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Many damaged electronic devices, such as computer motherboards, old cables, and plastic casings, are often thrown away. However, some of these components can still be reused. One creative way to reduce electronic waste is by transforming recycled electronic components into a mini Bluetooth speaker. This transformation shows that electronic waste can become a useful and valuable product.

The transformation begins when usable electronic components are collected from unused electronic devices. The components are cleaned and sorted based on their functions. A Bluetooth speaker module, speaker driver, battery, switch, and charging module are then assembled with recycled materials such as an old casing or decorative electronic parts. During this process, the components are connected carefully until they function as a complete speaker.

After all the components are assembled, the speaker is tested to ensure it works properly. The recycled electronic parts are arranged neatly to create an attractive appearance. Once the assembly is complete, the product becomes a mini Bluetooth speaker that can play music wirelessly through Bluetooth connection. The combination of reused materials and electronic components creates a functional and unique product.

As a result, recycled electronic components are transformed into a mini Bluetooth speaker that has both functional and economic value. The product can be used for listening to music while reducing the amount of electronic waste. This project also encourages people to recycle unused electronic materials into useful products. Therefore, reusing electronic waste is one way to support a cleaner and more sustainable environment
